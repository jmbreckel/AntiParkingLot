Prompt Parking Lot — Help v1.1.0

🛡️ Zero-Interruption Scratchpad

Capture follow-up ideas anytime while Antigravity is busy working. Everything you type, speak, or paste stays local on disk and never interrupts or queues behind an active agent run until you click a Send button. Unsent drafts and screenshots auto-save after 450 ms.

📷 Screenshots & 🎤 Voice Dictation

  • Screenshots: Press Ctrl+V right after taking a snip (Win+Shift+S), drag & drop images onto the composer (or an active edit card), or click 📷 Image. Click any thumbnail to inspect full-size. You can park a screenshot with or without text.
  • Voice Dictation: Click 🎤 Voice (Alt+V) for built-in neural/offline speech-to-text, or press Win+H (FnFn on Mac) for OS dictation.

🚀 Sending Ideas When Ready (Side Pane)

  • ▶ Send Here: Sends the prompt (plus notes & attached screenshot paths) straight into your current conversation.
  • ↗ New Chat: Starts a separate conversation in this project immediately—even while your current chat is still busy.
  • 📎 Attach: Attaches one or more parked ideas as context onto your next chat message.
  • Batch Combine: Check multiple cards to merge and send them together as a numbered prompt (▶ Send (N) / ↗ New Chat (N)).

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

Ctrl+EnterPark current idea (Cmd+Enter on Mac) Ctrl+VPaste clipboard screenshot into composer or edit box Alt+VStart / stop voice dictation F1 or ?Toggle this Help window (Esc to close)

💾 Local Data Storage

Stored locally in ~/.gemini/config/plugin-data/anti-parking-lot/ (parking_lot.json + screenshots/). Deleting an idea automatically removes its unreferenced screenshot files.
